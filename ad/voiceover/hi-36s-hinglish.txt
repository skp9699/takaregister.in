Kya aaj bhi kapde ke kaam ka hisaab notebook aur purane registeron mein rakhte ho?

Ek click par bata sakte ho ki kaunse godown mein kitna kilo yarn, aur kaunsi dyeing mein kitne taake pade hain?

Ab kapde ka hisaab notebook mein nahi, screen par hoga.

Yarn khareedne se lekar finish kapda bechne tak — kitne kilo, kitne taake, kitne metre.

Aur maal kitne dino se wahin phansa pada hai, ye bhi dikhega.

Aapko jo chahiye bataiye, agle update mein jodne ki koshish karte hain.

Stock sahi, toh business sahi. WhatsApp par message kijiye.
