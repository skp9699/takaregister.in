# Meta copy — Taka Register reel

Hinglish, because the audio is Hindi and the buyers read Latin faster than
Devanagari on a phone. Trade words stay as the trade says them: karigar, taka,
godown.

Two different things below. An organic Reel gets one caption field; a paid ad
in Ads Manager gets three separate fields plus a button.

---

in the first line. 2,200 character limit overall; this is well inside it.

scroll past it; these five are the ones an actual weaver or trader follows.

## Notes

- The shortage line — 600 kilo out where 500 should have gone, 950 metres back
  where 1000 should have — is the strongest thing in the ad. It is the one claim
  no competitor screenshot makes. Keep it in the primary text even when trimming.
- Do not put the phone number in the first 125 characters. It costs the hook and
  the button already carries it.
- Sample figures in the reel are illustrative. If a real party name ever appears
  in a screenshot, swap it before boosting.
